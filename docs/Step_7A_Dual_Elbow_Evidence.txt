## 8. Scope and Limitations
This experiment verifies two simulated REVOLUTE elbow joints under the same Smoothstep target schedule.

A small synchronization error indicates close agreement between the two simulated joint angles. It does not imply zero target-tracking error, perfectly mirrored physical motion, or general performance under different loads and control parameters.

The humanoid base is fixed. This experiment does not validate real Alpha 1S hardware, physical servo motors, walking balance, or complete coordinated body movement.

Recorded SHA-256 values identify file contents but do not independently prove a causal link between the source file and GUI recording.
